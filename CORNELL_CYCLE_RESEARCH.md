# Cornell plan v2 (2026-10-16): upper-priority revision

v2 keeps the four essential days and ROTC placement but adds side-delt and arm work to both
leg days (Tuesday: lateral raise + overhead triceps; Friday: lateral raise + preacher curl),
raises Wednesday rear-delt fly to 3 sets, and turns Sunday into an optional run **or**
pull + arms session. Calibration now happens only in cycle 1, and isolation lifts may take the
last set to 0-1 RIR in weeks 3-7. See `ENHANCEMENTS_V2.md` for the full table.

Why not 1 lower + 3 upper days? With weekly sets equal, training frequency barely changes
hypertrophy (Schoenfeld 2019; Pelland 2025), so the question is set allocation. Moving to
3 upper days would add only ~6 upper sets versus v2, cut quad volume by about a third, leave
legs untrained in any week Friday is missed, and put an upper session right after a PT push-up
day. Delts and arms recover quickly, so placing them on leg days captures most of the
upper-body benefit at little recovery cost.

The v1 description below is kept as the historical record.

# Cornell cycle: evidence, prescription, and critical review

Reviewed 2026-10-16. This is an individualized programming synthesis, not a
claim that one exercise or split is scientifically optimal for every person.
The old home-gym routine remains an immutable historical reference.

## Constraints and prescription

Four essential sessions, approximately 60-75 minutes including warm-up:

| Day | Role | Normal working sets (weeks 2-7) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Friday | Lower A | Leg press 3x8-12; seated curl 3x10-15; Bulgarian split squat 2x8-12/leg; calf press 3x10-15; cable crunch 2x10-15 |
| Saturday | Upper A | Low-incline DB press 3x6-10; assisted pull-up 3x6-10; chest-supported DB row 3x8-12; lateral raise 3x12-20; pec fly 2x10-15; overhead cable triceps 2x10-15; incline curl 2x10-15 |
| Sunday | Optional | Easy conversational jog/walk, initially 15-20 min; build toward 30 if recovery permits. Full rest is equally acceptable. |
| Monday | Fixed ROTC | Follow the instructor's running and calisthenics; no additional gym session. |
| Tuesday | Lower B | DB RDL 2x8-12; leg press 3x10-15; leg extension 2x10-15; seated curl 2x10-15; calf press 2x10-15; reverse crunch 2x10-15 |
| Wednesday | Upper B | Lat pulldown 3x8-12; chest press 3x8-12; seated row 2x8-12; lateral raise 3x12-20; rear-delt fly 2x12-20; incline curl 2x10-15 |
| Thursday | Fixed ROTC | Instructor's running and calisthenics. |

Week 1 is calibration: no more than two working sets per exercise, about 3 RIR.
Week 2 uses normal sets at 2-3 RIR. Weeks 3-7 use 1-2 RIR, except RDL and
Wednesday chest press stay at 2. Core stops when control breaks. Week 8 halves
sets, rounding up, at 3-4 RIR. A new eight-week block repeats without deleting
old logs. Rest 2-3 minutes on the main presses/pulls/leg press/split squat/RDL;
90-120 seconds for machine accessories, 60-90 for retained small isolation work.
Extend rest when breathing or performance has not recovered; do not race sets.

Progress by clean reps inside the prescribed range. Two confirmed comparable
sessions at the top range and target RIR support the smallest practical load
increase. Machines at a new facility establish a new load baseline. There are
no forced repetitions, extra-volume quests, or gym HIIT sessions.

## Evidence and equipment

- [Cornell's official Helen Newman equipment list](https://scl.cornell.edu/recreation/recreation/facility/helen-newman-fitness-center): leg press, seated curl, leg extension, calf press, chest/shoulder press, rear-delt/pec fly, pulldown, seated row, assisted chin/dip, high/low pulleys, adjustable benches, DBs 3-100 lb, racks, treadmills and bikes. No Smith/hack squat is assumed. Equipment occupancy and exact seat/stack setup are not guaranteed. The fitness center is upstairs near the elevator; broader hall/studio access is schedule-dependent.
- [Pelland et al., dose-response meta-regression](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41343037/): volume supports hypertrophy with diminishing returns; indirect work matters. This supports a moderate starting dose, not maximum possible sets. No universal "least effective exercise" ranking follows from this evidence.
- [Robinson et al., proximity to failure meta-regression](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38970765/): closer-to-failure training can support hypertrophy; uncertainty and exercise fatigue costs do not justify failure on every set.
- [Refalo et al., failure versus 1-2 RIR](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/pdf/10.1080/02640414.2024.2321021): similar hypertrophy in the studied setting with greater fatigue from failure. Applied conservatively to compounds.
- [Singer et al., rest-interval meta-analysis](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39205815/): rest beyond very short intervals can help; precise superiority of a single long interval is uncertain. Rest choices prioritize quality and a 75-minute session budget.
- [Schumann et al., concurrent training meta-analysis](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8891239/): endurance and lifting can coexist; explosive performance and same-session fatigue warrant care. This is not a guarantee that any running volume is interference-free.
- [Stone et al., ROTC two-mile training study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33235017/): structured intervals can improve two-mile performance. Because Monday/Thursday are fixed sessions, this plan does not independently add another hard interval day.
- [Morton et al., protein meta-analysis](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28698222/): practical protein range 1.6-2.2 g/kg/day, not a magic cutoff. Start around maintenance, use weekly weight/strength trends, and consider a small surplus if needed. No aggressive cut is prescribed.

## Independent critical review and revisions

1. **Consecutive days / ROTC:** lower lifting is Friday/Tuesday, not Sunday/Wednesday immediately before PT. Friday and Tuesday still follow PT, so hold load or use explicit cuts when fatigue is high. This is a compromise, not perfect recovery spacing.
2. **Chest and shoulders:** 8 direct chest sets plus variable ROTC push-ups; 6 lateral sets, 2 rear-delt sets plus rows. Redundant overhead pressing/dips were omitted to control anterior shoulder/triceps fatigue. Wednesday pressing stays at 2 RIR.
3. **Lower body:** about 10 quad, 7 hamstring, 5 calf, and 4 direct ab sets weekly, with glute work from presses/split squat/RDL. These are starting doses; ROTC calisthenic stimulus is not invented or automatically counted.
4. **Time budget:** 13/18/13/15 normal sets across the four days. Saturday is longest; optional noncompeting arm pairing and ranked explicit cuts address equipment queues. No claim that every busy-gym session fits exactly 75 minutes.
5. **Running fatigue:** a 15:50 two-mile test does not establish an easy pace or diagnose poor stamina. Use full-sentence pace, walking breaks, and a conservative opening in tests. Persistent unusual fatigue, dizziness, chest symptoms, or declining tolerance needs evaluation, not more intensity.
6. **Historical loads:** old performance is shown for exact unchanged movements. No October starting load is inferred from an August session. New machines get distinct comparison IDs. New completed sessions automatically supersede older references.
7. **Return to training:** reduced week-one work and planned week-eight deload are conservative choices under uncertain recent training exposure. The app does not diagnose overtraining from missing logs.
8. **Optional means optional:** no Sunday strength obligation, no optional-session requirement for weekly completion, no new quest/conditioning XP awards. Existing daily activity streak logic remains.

## Technical boundaries

New logs on/after 2026-10-16 use `programId: cornell-v1` and `programStartDate`.
Logs without that marker resolve to the legacy routine, even if reopened later.
The original settings start date and historical week fields are not reset.
Weekly activity summaries retain the existing user's week boundaries; Cornell
eight-week intensity uses its separate start date. The initial partial week need
not earn a perfect-week award. No historic XP compensation or reward inflation.
The original routine remains selectable as reference in Routine.

Browser timer audio repeats until dismissal while the page is active, includes
an explicit test button, mute and a visible dialog. Notification permission is
requested only by tapping the fallback control. Browser suspension, phone volume,
headphone routing and iOS restrictions prevent guaranteed locked-screen alarms.
